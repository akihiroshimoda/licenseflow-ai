# Legal Playbook

## Purpose

This document provides guidance on when Legal review is required for commercial licensing deals.

## Standard Deals

Legal review is not required when all of the following conditions apply:

- standard licence template is used
- contract term is three years or less
- no modification to intellectual property clauses
- no unusual liability terms
- use case is not regulated or restricted

## Mandatory Legal Review

Legal review is required when any of the following apply:

- automotive use
- healthcare or medical use
- government or defence customer
- contract term longer than three years
- customer requests changes to intellectual property ownership
- unlimited liability is requested
- sublicensing rights are requested
- multiple geographic regions are included

## Legal Escalation

High-risk or non-standard terms should not be automatically approved by an AI system.

The deal must be routed to a qualified Legal reviewer.
