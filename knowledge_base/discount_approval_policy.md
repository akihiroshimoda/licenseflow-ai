# Discount Approval Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the approval requirements for discounts applied to technology licensing deals.

## Approval Thresholds

### Discount below 10%

No additional commercial approval is required.

The Account Manager may proceed within standard commercial terms.

### Discount from 10% to 20%

Approval from the Sales Director is required.

The request must include:

- customer name
- product
- proposed discount
- contract term
- commercial rationale

### Discount above 20%

Approval from the VP Sales is required.

Commercial Operations must also review the deal before approval.

## Exception Handling

Any discount above 30% requires Finance review in addition to VP Sales approval.

Discounts above 40% are considered exceptional and must be escalated to the Chief Commercial Officer.
