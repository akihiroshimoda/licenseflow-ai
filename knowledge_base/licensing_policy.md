# Licensing Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard conditions for licensing company technology and intellectual property.

## Permitted Use

Licensed technology may only be used for the purposes defined in the commercial agreement.

Customers may not:

- sublicense the technology without written approval
- transfer the licence to another legal entity
- use the technology outside the agreed product scope
- reverse engineer protected intellectual property

## Contract Duration

Standard licensing agreements may run for one to three years.

Contracts exceeding three years require Commercial Operations review.

## Geographic Scope

Licensing agreements must define the authorised geographic region.

Requests covering multiple regions may require additional Legal review.

## Automotive Use

Technology used in automotive products requires Legal review because of additional regulatory, safety, and liability considerations.

## Restricted Use

Deals involving military, defence, sanctions-sensitive, or export-controlled applications require Compliance review before commercial approval.
