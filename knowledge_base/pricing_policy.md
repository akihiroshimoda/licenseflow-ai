# Pricing Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard pricing principles for technology licensing deals.

## Standard Pricing

The standard annual licence fee is based on the product catalogue.

Sales teams should use the listed base price as the starting point for all commercial discussions.

## Multi-Year Contracts

The following guidelines apply:

- 1-year contract: standard pricing
- 2-year contract: up to 5% commercial discount may be considered
- 3-year contract: up to 10% commercial discount may be considered
- Contracts longer than 3 years require Commercial Operations review

## Discount Principles

Discounts must not be used solely to accelerate deal closure.

Discounts should be supported by a clear commercial rationale, such as:

- strategic customer value
- expected volume
- multi-year commitment
- market entry opportunity

All discounts remain subject to the Discount Approval Policy.
